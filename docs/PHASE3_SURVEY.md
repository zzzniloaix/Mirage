## What §3 asks

1. **§3.1, the model.** A timeline that is a data structure with a query interface of its own, a picture on screen being only one use of it. Its one time base is the media's presentation time, with a wall-clock time kept beside it, and variants are compared on the reference variant's timeline. It merges six sources:
   - VMAF: each frame's score;
   - NetworkDebugger, the `av_log` interception: for each segment, when its HTTP request began, its first byte, its end, how long it took, its size and its HTTP status;
   - the parsing of HLS and DASH: `EXT-X-DISCONTINUITY`, where DASH periods begin and end, vendors' own tags;
   - SCTE-35: cue-out and cue-in, the splice point, the length of the ad;
   - the player and its ABR: each switch of variant, with the rung left and the rung reached;
   - the decoder: frames it dropped, errors in decoding, where the keyframes are.
2. **§3.2, attribution.** An API and a subcommand of the CLI that take an interval of low scores, given by the user or found on its own, and return the events that meet it in time, by source, each with its offset. A drop is a run of more than N frames over which a variant's score stays more than a threshold under a moving baseline of its own; the threshold is configurable, 6 VMAF points by the work order's suggestion. The work order's example answer: a drop that overlapped an SCTE-35 cue-out, a switch between two numbered rungs, and a segment request of 2.3 s. Acceptance is two test cases committed to the repository, which also serve the paper's evaluation: on a stream degraded by its encoding in one known segment, the query names that segment; on a stream whose score falls at an ad boundary, it names the cue.
3. **§3.3, the UI.** A timeline view that zooms and pans, with the per-frame curves and the events marked on them; clicking a low point seeks the player to that frame and shows what the query attributes to it; several variants' curves drawn together.

What Phase 3 writes also follows §7 (Phase 5's JSON output): a `timeline` array of `pts`, `wallclock`, `source`, `type` and `payload`, and an `attributions` array of `interval`, `drop_magnitude` and `overlapping_events`, in an output that carries `schema_version`, with its JSON Schema committed beside the code, and that records every option that changes a score. The constraints of the earlier phases hold: C++20 in the code's style; unit tests for each new feature; FFmpeg's libraries, never its programs; each setting both a command-line option and a config-file setting; any difference from the work order reported to the owner, no document edited to make the two agree. Encryption, metrics besides VMAF, scoring on servers or across machines, and live content are outside it; it covers ladders of VOD and files on disk.

## What the surveys measured and found

- **Frames to segments.** A packet's byte position against the sizes of the segments read before it named the right segment for 40 of 40 packets of the sources survey's fixture (`eval/phase3-survey/sources.md` §2), 200 of 200 packets on each of 4 rungs (`eval/phase3-survey/design-library/runs/map-by-pos-*.txt`) and 200 of 200 frames on each of 15 playlists of testgen's ladders, every frame's PTS agreeing with the report's (`eval/phase3-survey/design-evaluation/runs/03_segmap.txt`, `04_join_check.txt`). FFmpeg's own rule, PTS against EXTINF sums, got 37 of 40 on the fixture, 39 with a half-frame tolerance (`eval/phase3-survey/sources.md` §2), and 145 and 193 of 200 on testgen's ladders (`eval/phase3-survey/design-library/runs/map-by-pos-*.txt`). On fMP4 with `EXT-X-MAP`, ID3-timestamped audio, open GOPs and D-011's ladders: MEASUREMENT PENDING.
- **HTTP timing** (`eval/phase3-survey/sources.md` §3, §9). `mirage_vmaf`'s reads are paced by decoding and scoring: the playlist and three segments were open before the first packet came back (run `logprobe-file`). FFmpeg 8.1.1 exports the HTTP status through no AVOption ([http.c#L180-L206](https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/blob/n8.1.1/libavformat/http.c#L180-L206)), only in `av_log` lines or a failed open's error code, and its log callback is one per process (run `logprobe-concurrent`). A prototype of a sequential fetch after scoring measured 2313133 µs, 12032 bytes and `HTTP/1.0 200 OK` for a segment a local server answered 2.3 s late, and 578 to 3569 µs for the other segments (`eval/phase3-survey/design-evaluation/runs/06_session_fetch.txt`).
- **The tags FFmpeg skips.** The hls demuxer parses 11 tags and skips every other `#` line (`eval/phase3-survey/scte35.md` §7), `EXT-X-DISCONTINUITY` among them, and passes each segment's timestamps through: on testgen's ad ladder they jump by −2.960000 s before frame 75 and +3.040000 s before frame 125, and since the report breaks a discontinuity sequence only at a step back or a jump forward of more than 10 s (`src/VMAFAlign.h:114`), it has two sequences where the playlist has three (`eval/phase3-survey/testgen.md` §3.5). PTS alone can't key that timeline: 50 PTS values each name two reference frames (`eval/phase3-survey/design-evaluation/runs/09_axes.txt`).
- **SCTE-35** (`eval/phase3-survey/scte35.md`). D-070's plain cue tags (`EXT-X-CUE-OUT`, `EXT-X-CUE-OUT-CONT`, `EXT-X-CUE-IN`, `#EXT-OATCLS-SCTE35`) have no specification and vendors print their own forms, while `EXT-X-DATERANGE`'s SCTE-35 attributes are RFC 8216's (§2); three more signalings are in use (§2.6, §9); vendors disagree on whether a cue sits at its tag, at its `START-DATE` or at its message's splice time (§4). Of 79 published messages from 15 sources, 61 decode with a valid CRC_32 and no disagreement with their source, 9 have a bad CRC_32 and 9 don't decode (§5); 1 of the first 9 and 3 of the second are a library's intended negative tests (`eval/phase3-survey/review-3/runs/vector-status.txt`, from `eval/phase3-survey/scte35_evidence/runs/check_vectors.txt`), where the survey's summary says three of each (§0, item 5), an error the committed copy marks where it stands. The one public VOD stream with SCTE-35 found also carries it inside its segments, as ID3 (§8).
- **The synthetic ladders** (`eval/phase3-survey/testgen.md`, a prototype of D-069's content on FFmpeg's libraries only: `eval/phase3-survey/review-3/runs/testgen-library-only.txt`). Three rungs of eight 1 s segments at 25 fps (§2.3). On the degraded ladder `v270`'s segment 4 at QP 46 has a mean VMAF of 41.239 against 97.134 to 98.231 in its other segments, its frames more than 6 points below the pair's median exactly 100 to 124 (§3.1); on the ad ladder `v180`'s two ad segments score 27.409 and 27.380 against 90.966 to 91.978, its low frames 75 to 124, and frame 0, the first of a libvmaf context (§3.2); the controls have no drop. Each ladder made twice gave 29 of 29 files with the same SHA-256 (§3.3); generation took 2.022 to 2.313 s, 16.941 s unoptimized, and scoring 0.457 to 0.515 s (§3.4).
- **The report against §7** (`eval/phase3-survey/schema.md`). A ladder report has 22 top-level keys, 14 per pair and 6 per frame (§1.3, §1.4); no `schema_version`, `tool_version` or `git_commit`, no JSON Schema file, and the build no version (§1.7); `src/` has no JSON reader (§1.1). ## The design in brief

`eval/phase3-survey/design.md` merges a library-first design and one worked back from the paper's evaluation, and was revised three times, the third time by the lead against a third review's minor issues (its §1). Its sections:

- **Layers** (§3). A core of plain C++20 modules in namespace `timeline`, with libavutil but no libavformat, libavcodec, libvmaf, GL or ImGui; adapters, the only code that opens inputs (the demux pass, the session fetch); on top, two subcommands of `mirage_vmaf` and the player's panel. The scorer and the render and decode paths stay as they are unless an answer changes them (§3.6).
- **The time base** (§4; Q02). Every event sits at a reference frame, and intervals are compared on reference frames, as integers. Each event keeps its media key, the reference's discontinuity sequence and PTS, unique for all 200 frames of the ad ladder where PTS alone had 150 values (`eval/phase3-survey/design-library/runs/axis-ad.txt`). Offsets are printed in the seconds Q02 picks: the agent recommends the reference's playlist time, with the frame index times the frame duration where there is no segment map, the two compared at every frame (§4.2; `eval/phase3-survey/design/runs/seconds-check.txt`).
- **Sources** (§5). The pairs' curves; each scored media playlist parsed; a demux pass mapping every scored frame to its segment by byte position, checked against the report's PTS, with keyframes and corrupt packets; D-070's tags, a splice_info_section decoder and one cue interface for every carrier; sessions as scripted plans of segment ranges (D-068), timed by a fetch through `NetworkLogger`; decode errors only if the owner allows the analyzer to record them (Q09).
- **Queries and detection** (§6). `timeline::attribute()` lists the events meeting a half-open interval of a pair's or a session's curve, grouped by §3.1's sources, with offsets in frames and seconds; `timeline::detect()` finds drops under a trailing median; a session's drops are its pairs' drops clipped to the spans that played them (Q22). The prototype's trailing median found both acceptance drops exactly, with none in either control, for every T from 3 to 40 and N from 1 to 24 of its grid, at W = 10, 25, 50 and 100 (`eval/phase3-survey/design-evaluation/runs/05_detection_sweep.txt`).
- **The document** (§8; Q01, Q04 to Q06). A separate, versioned timeline document per build, naming its ladder report by the report's SHA-256 and by a digest of its scores that survives redaction, and copying no score; `schema_version` with `docs/schema/mirage-timeline-1.schema.json`. The prototype's documents for 200-frame ladders were 8577 to 14470 bytes, with 32 to 44 events (`eval/phase3-survey/design-evaluation/runs/11_doc_sizes.txt`).
- **The CLI** (§7; Q31). `mirage_vmaf timeline` builds a document from a ladder report and, optionally, a session plan; `mirage_vmaf attribute` queries it without reading media. Every option is also a config-file setting (D-030); W and N have no default until the owner gives one (Q26).
- **The tests** (§9). A unit-test file per module, nothing downloaded; §3.2's two acceptance tests on ladders from a port of testgen's generator, with T = 6, W = 50 and N = 12 pinned inside the measured region (P-R); the work order's example on a scripted session with a delayed segment; `eval/timeline/` to reproduce it all.
- **The UI plan** (§10; Q36). `eval/phase3-survey/ui.md`'s panel, its loader and math free of ImGui and tested in `mirage_tests`, on the same query API. By the agent's reading nothing recommended touches the render or decode path; §10 is the written plan D-007 asks for.

**The reviews.** The first two (`eval/phase3-survey/review-adversarial/`, `eval/phase3-survey/review-2/`) each asked for a revision (`eval/phase3-survey/design/revision-1.md`, `revision-2.md`). The third (`eval/phase3-survey/review-3/review.md`) found the design ready for round 1's questions once seven minor corrections are made, with no blocker and no major issue: (1) the PTS test of the design's §4.2 can't see a frame lost at the start of a playlist discontinuity sequence (`eval/phase3-survey/review-3/runs/seconds-check-sequence-start.txt`); (2) WP00b wrote into headers it didn't list, one of them round-1 WP09a's; (3) eight packages' Blocked-by lists missed answers that change them; (4) Q16 counted 3 intended negative tests where the record has 1; (5) §4.2 had RFC 8216 allow whole-second EXTINF only below compatibility version 3, where its §4.3.2.1 allows them at every version; (6) WP00a's list of new files said less than its text; (7) WP15b depended on WP15a alone, though its documents come last. Revision 3, the lead agent's, makes the seven (`eval/phase3-survey/design/revision-3.md`) and was not reviewed again; `eval/phase3-survey/design/runs/revise3-reproduce.txt` shows that revision 2 as first committed, with its edits applied, renders byte-identical to the design committed. The check of this document's draft (`eval/phase3-survey/review-docs/verify.md`) found three statements false against their records and six minor issues: they are corrected here and in `docs/PHASE3_STATUS.md` (the controls have low frames but no drop; the detection sweep's exact region is T from 3 to 40 and N from 1 to 24; only D-070's plain cue tags lack a specification; the fixture's counts are the sources survey's; §3.2's baseline is a moving one, "trailing" being the design's choice, Q23; `SegmentWatch` keeps no byte count), and in the records (the model's name removed from two of them, D-060; the seven quotations of third-party text of 25 words or more put in other words; `eval/phase3-survey/README.md`).

And `docs/VMAF_GAP_REPORT.md:230-231` gives the Decoder a dropped-frame counter that is the render loop's `total_dropped` (`src/main.cpp:1751`; `eval/phase3-survey/sources.md` §12): reported, not edited.
