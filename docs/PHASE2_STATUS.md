## Measurements

- **M1**, the machine: an Apple M3 Pro with 12 physical cores, 6 performance and 6 efficiency cores (`eval/phase2-survey/machine.txt`).
- **M5**, discontinuities (`eval/phase2-survey/m5/record.txt`): FFmpeg's hls demuxer gives each segment's own timestamps, unchanged at an `EXT-X-DISCONTINUITY`; D-028's rule then stopped pairing at the tag, 30 pairs and the 60 frames after it failed. With D-046 every frame pairs, 60 pairs and no failure (`m5-d046/record.txt`), and so with D-050 (`m5-d050/record.txt`).
- **M5's gaps** (`eval/phase2-survey/m5-gaps/record.txt`; P-070): playlists that lack about 12 s of segments, without a tag. Where both inputs lack the same segments, D-046's pairing pairs all 719 frames, where D-028's rule paired 240; where they lack different ones, it scores 359 pairs, of which 119 pair a reference frame with the distorted frame 12.012 s later, and nothing in the report sets them apart. With D-050, where they lack different ones, 240 pairs and no pair of frames at different times, and where they lack the same ones, all 719 frames pair (`m5-gaps-d050/record.txt`; P-073).
- **M2, M3**, before the pool (`eval/phase2-survey/m2-m3/record.txt`; P-071): one pair of the local advanced sample, 300 frame pairs, peaked at 292995072 bytes of resident memory in 4.20 s with two 1080p60 inputs and at 272957440 bytes in 4.19 s against the 480x270 rung at 30 fps; the ladder's 7 pairs one after another at 304955392 bytes in 28.91 s, on battery power.
- **The pool** (`eval/ladder/advanced-local-300/`; P-071): at the sizes 1, 2, 4 and the default, 11, the peaks were 304529408, 581894144, 1111425024 and 2009759744 bytes and the wall times 33.146, 20.173, 13.558 and 14.553 s, one run each on battery power. Each pair keeps libvmaf's 4 threads (D-044), and in M2 a pair used 17.40 s of CPU time in 4.20 s.
- **One pair's memory** (`eval/ladder/advanced-local-pair-memory/record.txt`, the `warm_up.txt` of `advanced-local-300-mem1g-dfed5a3/`): 284639232 bytes after 30 frame pairs, 293208064 after 300, 301056000 over all 720 of the copy; over every frame from Apple's CDN, 345407488 bytes, in 419.80 s (`eval/ladder/advanced-cdn-pair-full/record.txt`).
- **The ladder over every frame** (`eval/ladder/advanced-cdn-full-af17121/`; P-073): from Apple's CDN at the default size with a limit of 1 GiB, 1907.7 s, 3 pairs at once and a peak of 1096171520 bytes.
